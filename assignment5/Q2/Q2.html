Welcome to Agra

Agra is a famous historical city in Uttar Pradesh, India. It is known throughout the world for the beautiful Taj Mahal.

History of Agra

Agra was an important city during the Mughal period. Many remarkable monuments were constructed in and around the city during this time.

Popular Places

Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is one of India's most famous monuments.

Agra Fort

Agra Fort is a large historical fort built using red sandstone.

Mehtab Bagh

Mehtab Bagh is a beautiful garden located across the Yamuna River from the Taj Mahal.

Famous Food

Agra is popular for its petha, bedai, kachori, and other traditional dishes.
