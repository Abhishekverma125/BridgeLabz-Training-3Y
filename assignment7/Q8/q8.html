CSS Box Model

Content, padding, border and margin

Parts of the Box Model

Content
  • Blue — Content: Text or images inside the box.
  • Green — Padding: Space around the content.
  • Brown — Border: The edge around the padding.
  • Orange — Margin: Space outside the border.
